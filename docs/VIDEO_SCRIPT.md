# Aeonith: 3-minute demo video script

Record at 1440×900 in the real PayPal **sandbox** (not simulated mode). Hide notifications. Voiceover can be AI-generated. Keep it **under 3:00**; judges stop watching there.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Home hero: the 3D claw holding the card, the ember gradient, "Your Money Held Until It's Done". Move the mouse so the claw turns. | "Home repair was Tennessee's number-one consumer complaint last year. You get one quote, you pay a deposit, and you hope. Aeonith flips that." |
| 0:15–0:35 | Type in the hero box: *"Water heater leaking, fix by Friday, max $400, licensed only."* The agent fills the form. Create the request. | "Say what you need and the most you'll pay. Claude turns it into a structured job. Your limit stays private." |
| 0:35–0:55 | Click **Hold $400 with PayPal**, log in as the sandbox buyer, approve, and return. The claw **grips** the card, which shows "$400 · held, not paid". | "You approve one PayPal hold. It's reserved, not paid to anyone." |
| 0:55–1:20 | Click **Invite pros' agents to bid**: sparks circle the claw and the sealed cards show scrambling SHA-256 hashes. Cut to **Pros**: "Buyer price limits: never sent to providers". | "Pros' AI agents compete with sealed bids. Each one commits a cryptographic fingerprint. Nobody, not even us, can read a bid early, and they never see your limit." |
| 1:20–1:45 | **Unseal bids & pick winner**: the cards flip open, the unlicensed $312 bid is filtered out, Lone Star wins at **$360**, and the AI promise check appears. | "Bids are revealed and checked against their fingerprints. Plain code picks the best licensed offer, and Claude checks the winner's promises." |
| 1:45–2:10 | **Pro: start job · get code** shows the amber code ticket. Upload a photo of the finished job with the code on paper. The checks appear and the amber scan line sweeps the card. | "Money moves only on proof. The pro photographs the work with a one-time code. Code rejects reused photos; AI vision reads the code and checks the job." |
| 2:10–2:30 | **Test: let an agent overcharge** shows the toast "Policy gate blocked". Then **Confirm**, then **Release payment**: the claw opens and the card drops away. The receipt shows $360 charged, $40 back, and the payout to the pro. | "An agent can't overcharge: a policy gate checks every PayPal call. Release: PayPal captures $360, returns $40, and pays the pro with Payouts." |
| 2:30–2:45 | **Together** job: neighbours' holds, group price $118 against a $160 solo quote; Sam's $90 is released, never charged. | "With neighbours it gets better: one trip, a lower price for everyone, and nobody pays more than their own solo quote." |
| 2:45–3:00 | **Trust log**: "Chain intact", then the hero again. | "Every step is in a tamper-evident log. Aeonith: compete, hold, prove, pay. Built on PayPal and Claude." |

**Must be visible:** the word **PayPal** (sandbox approval page and the receipt), **Claude / AI** (intake, promise check, vision), and money actually moving (sandbox IDs on the receipt).

**Also show:** one failure path (overcharge blocked, or a reused photo failing proof).
